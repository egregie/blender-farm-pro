## What was actually checked

- **Scene** — every label row's bounds read out of the built scene via the driver's `--dump-scene`, checked against the face edges and against the row below. This is the pass that can see a text defect at all: a row that overruns the face or collides with its neighbour leaves the silhouette completely unchanged, so the pixel pass is blind to it.
